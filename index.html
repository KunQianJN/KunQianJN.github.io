宋校能,钱琨,后烜,等.基于三重注意力机制与空间分层池化策略的膝外翻步态识别模型设计与应用研究[J]. 生物医学工程学杂志,2025,42(5):994-1004. PDF

提出一种基于深度学习的儿童膝外翻智能识别方法，通过融合多维度步态特征，实现膝外翻的精准识别与早期筛查，为智能医疗诊断和儿童健康评估提供技术支持。

Qian K, Shen J, Wang S, et al. SiamUF: SiamCar based small UAV tracker using dense U-shape deep features in near infrared videos[J]. Optics and Lasers in Engineering, 2025, 186: 108825. PDF

Chen J, Qian K*, Song W, et al. SiamTU: Hyperspectral video tracking against appearance changes using improved SiameGAT with adaptive template updating[J]. Infrared Physics & Technology, 2025, 147: 105811. PDF
